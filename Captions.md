* Homemade-style food
* Convenience

* Natural and conversational language
* Clear but non-salesy CTAs

## Content Guidelines

* Keep captions conversational and relatable.
* Avoid exaggerated claims.
* Avoid fake customer testimonials.
* Focus on realistic customer problems.
* Keep the CTA simple and natural.
* Adapt hashtags according to the platform.
* Do not claim specific product benefits unless they are verified by the business.
