# Call-To-Action (CTA) Content

## Campaign: Divine Cuisine

**Business:** Divine Cuisine  
**Location:** Mangalore, Karnataka  
**Product:** Homemade-style lunch and dinner meals

## CTA Objective

The CTAs are designed to encourage the audience to take a simple next action after watching the UGC advertisement.

The CTA style is:

- Natural
- Friendly
- Conversational
- Action-oriented
- Non-salesy

---

# CTA Strategy

Different CTAs are used depending on the campaign objective.

### Engagement

Used to encourage interaction:

- Save this
- Send this to a friend
- Tag your friend

### Consideration

Used when the audience is interested but not ready to purchase:

- Check out Divine Cuisine
- Keep Divine Cuisine in mind

### Conversion

Used to encourage the audience to try the product:

- Give Divine Cuisine a try
- Try it once
- Check out Divine Cuisine for your next meal

The CTA should match the platform, audience and objective of the advertisement.
