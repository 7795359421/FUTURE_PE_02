# UGC Advertisement Scripts

## Divine Cuisine

**Business:** Divine Cuisine  
**Location:** Mangalore, Karnataka  
**Tagline:** Homely Food, Divine Bite  
**Product:** Homemade-style lunch and dinner meals  
**Target Audience:** College students, working professionals and office employees

---

## Script 1 – Homemade Food

### Hook
Tired of eating outside food every day?

### Script
As a student, I don't always have time to cook. Between classes, assignments and everything else, cooking can be difficult. That's when I tried Divine Cuisine. Their homemade-style meals are convenient and give me that comforting food feeling without spending time in the kitchen.

### CTA
If you're in Mangalore and missing homemade food, give Divine Cuisine a try!
