# UGC Framework

Every script follows the same conversion-focused structure:

**Hook → Problem → Personal Experience → Solution → Benefits → CTA**

### Why this framework works

- **Hook:** Captures attention in the first few seconds.
- **Problem:** Shows a relatable customer pain point.
- **Personal Experience:** Makes the content feel authentic.
- **Solution:** Introduces Divine Cuisine naturally.
- **Benefits:** Explains why the product is useful.
- **CTA:** Gives the viewer a clear next action.

## Content Style

The scripts are designed to sound:

- Natural
- Conversational
- Authentic
- Relatable
- Customer-focused
- Non-salesy

## Recommended Video Length

**20–30 seconds per UGC advertisement.**
