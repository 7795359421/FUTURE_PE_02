# Video Ideas — Divine Cuisine

## 1. Project Overview

**Business Name:** Divine Cuisine
**Business Type:** Home-based Cloud Kitchen
**Location:** Mangalore, Karnataka
**Tagline:** *Homely Food, Divine Bite*

Divine Cuisine provides homemade-style lunch and dinner meals for people who want convenient, comforting food without having to spend time preparing it themselves.

The video campaign focuses on authentic UGC-style content that feels like a genuine customer recommendation rather than a traditional advertisement.

---

## 2. Target Audience

The video content is designed for:

* College students
* Working professionals
* Office employees
* People living away from home
* People who don't have enough time or energy to cook

---

## 3. Main Customer Problem

Busy students and working professionals often have limited time and energy to prepare meals.

They may:

* Skip meals because of busy schedules
* Depend on outside food frequently
* Feel tired after college or work
* Miss homemade food while living away from home
* Want a convenient but comforting meal

---

# 4. UGC Video Ideas

## Video Idea 1 — “Too Tired to Cook”

**Concept:**
Show a working professional returning home after a long day and realizing they don't have the energy to cook.

**Hook:**
“POV: You're hungry, tired, and absolutely not in the mood to cook.”

**Story:**
The person comes home, looks at the kitchen, checks their phone and chooses Divine Cuisine for dinner.

**Ending:**
They enjoy the homemade-style meal and recommend it naturally to the camera.

**CTA:**
“If you're in Mangalore, give Divine Cuisine a try.”

# 5. Visual Style

The videos should maintain a realistic UGC appearance.

### Recommended Style

* Vertical 9:16 format
* Smartphone-style camera
* Natural lighting
* Casual clothing
* Realistic Indian home environment
* Natural facial expressions
* Simple camera movements
* Close-up food shots
* Natural eating reactions
* Minimal staging
* Conversational presentation

The videos should look like they were recorded by an actual customer rather than produced as a high-budget commercial.

---

# 6. Video Structure

Each short-form video should follow this structure:

**0–3 seconds — Hook**
Grab the viewer's attention with a relatable statement.

**3–7 seconds — Problem**
Show the customer's everyday problem.

**7–15 seconds — Personal Experience**
Show how the customer normally deals with the problem.

**15–22 seconds — Solution**
Introduce Divine Cuisine naturally.

**22–27 seconds — Benefits**
Show convenience, homemade-style food and comfort.

**27–30 seconds — CTA**
Ask the viewer to try, check out or remember Divine Cuisine.

# 8. Voiceover Style

The voiceover should sound like a real customer talking to a friend.

### Avoid:

* “Introducing the revolutionary Divine Cuisine!”
* “The number one food service in Mangalore!”
* “You absolutely must buy this product!”

### Prefer:

* “I honestly didn't feel like cooking.”
* “I started trying Divine Cuisine on busy days.”
* “It just makes lunch and dinner a little easier.”
* “If you're in Mangalore, give it a try.”

The goal is to make the audience feel that someone is sharing a genuine personal experience.
