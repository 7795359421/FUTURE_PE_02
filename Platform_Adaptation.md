# Platform Adaptation

## Instagram Reels

**Format:** Vertical short-form video

**Duration:** 20–30 seconds

**Style:** Fast, authentic and conversational

**Focus:** Strong hook + relatable story + clear CTA

### Example

"Tired of eating outside food every day? I found a better option..."


## YouTube Shorts

**Format:** Vertical short-form video

**Duration:** 20–30 seconds

**Style:** Fast-paced and engaging

**Focus:** Strong opening + quick value + CTA

### Example

"If you're a student in Mangalore and don't have time to cook, watch this."


## Facebook

**Format:** Short video + caption

**Style:** Friendly and slightly more informative

**Focus:** Product benefits + convenience + CTA

### Example Caption

"Looking for convenient homemade-style meals in Mangalore? Divine Cuisine provides comforting meal options for busy students, professionals and families."


## Content Adaptation Strategy

The core UGC message remains the same across platforms, while the presentation is adjusted according to the platform.

- Instagram: Focus on visual appeal and a strong first few seconds.
- YouTube Shorts: Focus on fast storytelling and retention.
- Facebook: Provide slightly more context through the caption.
