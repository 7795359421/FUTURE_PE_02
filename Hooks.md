# UGC Ad Hooks

## Campaign: Divine Cuisine

**Business:** Divine Cuisine  
**Product:** Homemade-style lunch and dinner meals  
**Location:** Mangalore, Karnataka  
**Target Audience:** College students, working professionals and people living away from home

---

## 10 Scroll-Stopping UGC Hooks

### Hook 1 — POV

> “POV: You're hungry, tired, and absolutely not in the mood to cook.”

**Angle:** Relatable busy-day situation

---

### Hook 2 — Food Fatigue

> “Does anyone else get tired of eating outside food every single day?”

**Angle:** Problem-focused

---

### Hook 3 — College Students

> “College students, this one is for you if cooking feels impossible during busy days.”

**Angle:** Target-audience specific

---

### Hook 4 — After Work

**Angle:** Working professional problem

### Hook 5 — Curiosity

| Emotional Connection | Hook 6, 8 |

| Local Targeting | Hook 10 |

## Hook Guidelines

- Keep the opening natural and conversational.
- Address a real customer problem.
- Avoid sounding like a traditional advertisement.
- Create curiosity or emotional connection.
- Make the first few seconds attention-grabbing.
- Adapt the hook according to the target audience and platform.
